01 / THE PROJECT

Physical infrastructure.
Fictional business scenario.

Tapajós Inovações is a fictional organization created to contextualize an enterprise network modernization project. The underlying laboratory is physical: equipment was installed, network segments were configured, services were deployed and operating evidence was collected.

ENTERPRISE NOC · FROM SCRATCH

From physical infrastructure to network operations

Enterprise NOC — From Scratch documents the path from a network rack and switch configuration to departmental segmentation, Linux-based services and observability. It is a technical case study, not a production service or a live dashboard.

⌁
Real laboratory, fictional clientThe corporate business requirements are simulated; the photographed equipment, host inventories and monitoring captures come from the actual training environment.
✓
Documented, not exaggeratedScreenshots are historical records. A dashboard reading is not a guarantee of uptime, tested security isolation or complete service coverage.

Four challenges that shape the scenario

The simulated business starts with connectivity, Wi-Fi, VoIP and visibility problems. The project maps technical responses to each requirement.

The T568A connection pattern

QUESTIONS & CONTEXT

Scope and
technical evidence.

Is Tapajós Inovações a real company?

No. The company and its modernization requirements form a simulated business scenario. The equipment and screenshots document a real physical training laboratory.

Is this a live NOC dashboard?

No. The website is a static technical showcase on GitHub Pages. The monitoring images are dated snapshots, not live telemetry.

Does the project prove every ACL and Wi-Fi authentication test?

No. The physical design and switch port assignments are documented. Router ACL enforcement and some wireless settings are not represented as tested when no corresponding record exists.

Were all hosts healthy in the screenshots?

No. Historical monitoring records include unavailable agents and unsupported items. The site shows those findings rather than altering them.
